# Persistent candidate exclusions

These are configurable screening defaults for adult solid-tumour supervisor searches. Apply them to new searches in both `cv_aligned_oncology` and `lung_cancer` unless the user explicitly overrides a named person or defined field for the current case. Naming a school, permitting broader omics research or supplying a CV does not itself waive a gate. Updating this skill does not rewrite historical cases.

## Recruitment status is not an exclusion gate

Failure to verify `open_current`, absence of a public posting, a closed call, an inaccessible vacancy portal, `no_public_evidence`, `route_confirmed_no_vacancy`, or `unverified` recruitment must never remove an otherwise eligible candidate or lower topic, supervision, or A–D classification. Preserve the person and all research evidence; change only the opportunity/recruitment fields. A dated PI/lab reply that no place or capacity is available for a stated intake may create a scope-limited outreach hold with a recheck condition, but it is not a permanent research-direction exclusion. Keep private-reply metadata in an external correspondence ledger; if needed in the research package, retain only a minimal note/caution and do not convert the private message into a public source. If the same reply separately establishes retirement, a move, or loss of supervision eligibility, assess that independent fact under the role/supervision rules and preserve historical evidence in coverage/source audit.

## Establish oncology relevance for each recommendation

Before the exclusion checks, establish a person's actual current cancer connection rather than equating a transferable method with oncology. Read the current official person/lab/project page and retain:

- **Disease and material:** named tumour type, malignant-cell/tumour model, adult solid-tumour/pan-cancer cohort, tumour microenvironment, or an explicit cancer-incidence/risk endpoint.
- **Scientific question:** cancer initiation/progression, invasion/metastasis, tumour immune escape, treatment response/resistance, tumour-specific molecular mechanisms, cancer biomarkers or cancer risk. Healthy-tissue immune regulation, normal development, generic ageing/metabolism and non-cancer diseases are not sufficient merely because their methods transfer.
- **Current person role:** owns/leads the stated cancer programme or is explicitly involved in a named current cancer project. Institutional names, generic programme pages, a collaborator's role and paper affiliations cannot establish the person's own current programme.
- **Literature cross-check:** verify author identity, affiliation, actual study disease/models, year and author/project role using recent primary papers, normally the past five years. A review, cancer mentioned only as background, a method benchmark, or an old ordinary coauthorship does not establish a sustained current cancer line. Missing recent papers is a visible evidence gap, not automatic disproof when the official current programme is explicit.

For A/B/C records, use existing `notes` for `Oncology relevance: direct_current` or `project_participation_current`, followed by disease, question, exact role, check date and supporting source IDs. Use the usual research and claim-level evidence arrays as well; do not create a new schema field. Current project participation can support a conditional collaborator/platform assessment, but must not be presented as independent programme ownership.

Record `historical_only`, `unverified` or `not_oncology` in sourced coverage notes when current relevance cannot be established. Confirmed non-oncology and unresolved-relevance leads stay outside the recommended roster. A historical-only lead may remain solely as an explicit D-tier historical warning under the existing evidence rules, provided no institutional or research-direction exclusion applies. Positive cancer-keyword checks in scripts do not replace this manual audit.

## Decide from current programme evidence

Inspect the person's current official laboratory, research-group, department and project pages, then use recent primary research to verify author identity and the actual programme balance. Apply two different thresholds:

- **Disease-focus threshold — paediatric oncology and haematologic malignancies:** exclude only when current person-specific evidence shows that the unwanted disease area is the **main or predominant focus of the person's overall current research programme**. Indicators can include the lab's stated central mission, the preponderance of current named projects or trainee topics, and recurrent PI-led/senior/corresponding work. Do not decide from a mechanical paper count, a department/service label or one grant. Retain an otherwise eligible candidate when paediatric or haematologic work is co-equal and parallel, secondary, occasional, collaborative, or one component of a broader pan-cancer programme.
- **Modality threshold — nuclear medicine and radiomics/core tumour imaging:** retain the existing stricter rule and exclude when the modality is a core or explicitly parallel current research line. The separate engineering/computing institutional gate also remains unchanged.

A department name, a technique keyword, an old project or one isolated collaboration paper is insufficient by itself. General terms such as pan-cancer, immune cells or multi-omics do not establish either an excluded disease focus or its dominance. When a material dominance question cannot be resolved, do not assert or enact exclusion. Retain the otherwise eligible candidate, label the dated audit `dominance_unresolved_retained`, make the caution visible and prioritize rechecking.

| Default exclusion | Evidence to check |
|---|---|
| Paediatric/childhood oncology | Exclude only when cancers in children/adolescents, paediatric solid tumours, or paediatric cancer-specific mechanisms/treatment are the main or predominant focus of the person's overall current programme. Retain an otherwise eligible candidate when paediatric work is co-equal and parallel, secondary or collaborative. A tumour label such as sarcoma alone does not establish paediatric focus or dominance. |
| Haematologic malignancies | Exclude only when leukemia, lymphoma, multiple myeloma, MDS, myeloproliferative neoplasm or another blood-cancer programme is the main or predominant focus of the person's overall current programme. Retain an otherwise eligible candidate when blood-cancer work is co-equal and parallel, secondary or collaborative. A haematology department, normal immune-cell biology or CAR-T for adult solid tumours alone is insufficient. |
| Nuclear medicine | Core or parallel radiopharmaceutical/tracer development, radionuclide diagnosis or therapy, PET/SPECT tumour imaging, radioligand treatment, PRRT or nuclear-medicine theranostics. An occasional PET scan used to stage a clinical cohort does not establish this focus. |
| Radiomics and core clinical/molecular tumour imaging | Core or parallel CT/MRI/PET radiomics or radiogenomics, quantitative clinical image analysis, imaging biomarkers, clinical imaging AI, or dedicated molecular tumour-imaging development/monitoring. Apply the programme-level boundary rather than requiring the literal word radiomics. |
| Engineering/computing/standalone mathematical or data-methods appointments | Apply the institutional gate in `search-playbook.md`: engineering-only, computer-science-only, standalone AI/data-science/data-analytics, and pure mathematics/statistical-methods appointments lacking a verified eligible biomedical/medical/cancer appointment stay outside the roster. A collaboration or paper affiliation does not create a current joint appointment. |

## Retain the intended molecular/omics scope

- Tumour immunity, molecular/cell biology, translational genetics/genomics and multi-omics in adult solid tumours may qualify. A generic pan-cancer programme requires an explicit accepted axis and an actual disease-focus check; inclusion of some paediatric or haematologic tumours does not itself trigger exclusion unless that area is shown to be the main/dominant focus.
- Digital/computational pathology, pathomics and histological image analysis within eligible medical/cancer units are not automatically covered by the radiomics exclusion. Preserve this boundary unless the user explicitly extends the exclusion to those fields; a verified parallel clinical-radiomics line still triggers exclusion.
- Microscopy, live-cell imaging, in-vivo experimental observation, imaging mass cytometry, MALDI imaging and spatial transcriptomics/proteomics are not excluded merely because they contain image data. Judge the scientific question and whether clinical/molecular tumour imaging itself is a core programme.
- Medical/cancer bioinformatics, clinical biostatistics and computational omics in verified eligible units may qualify. Original algorithm development or computation skills alone do not waive the institutional gate.
- Radiotherapy, radiation biology or radiosensitisation without an excluded nuclear-medicine or core imaging programme is not automatically excluded.
- Where evidence cannot resolve a material main-focus question for paediatric/haematologic disease, retain the otherwise eligible candidate with a dated `dominance_unresolved_retained` caution; do not delete, exclude or downgrade solely for that uncertainty. For unresolved core/parallel-line concerns involving nuclear medicine/imaging, keep the lead outside the recommendation roster with a dated unresolved audit and `blocked_unverified` coverage where applicable. Missing evidence is not proof that the unwanted programme exists.

## Keep the exclusion auditable

Use existing schema-1.2 fields; no new candidate-fit or exclusion field is needed.

1. State the active defaults and any explicit exception in `case.limitations`.
2. Put each excluded/unresolved name, actual unit, check date, decision, reason and source IDs in the relevant `coverage[].notes`; cite the inspected pages in that row's `sourceIds`. Preserve each page's real authority, primary type and supported claim domains, including `research` when the actual direction is the exclusion evidence.
3. Excluded people are not roster entities: leave them out of `records.json`, all A/B/C/D counts, `coverage[].candidateIds`, and discovery-pass additions. Do not leave their former candidate IDs in `sources[].subjectIds` or `personRoles`. Bind only existing school IDs explicitly supported by the page; retain the excluded name and faithful evidence in the source claim. A school-bound research exclusion source without retained person roles has `topicRelevance: background`; a source without `research` uses `not_applicable`.
4. If the inspected unit exists but no eligible candidate remains, mark it `searched`, not `not_present`. Preserve other eligible people in the same branch.
5. Recheck oncology relevance and both exclusion gates for additions from papers, grants, trainee lists and collaborators, and audit final counts/references before rendering. Structural validation does not independently infer a person's disease focus or institutional eligibility.
6. For a retained otherwise eligible candidate with co-equal parallel, secondary or collaborative paediatric/haematologic work, record `Disease-focus exclusion audit: parallel_secondary_retained`, the programme balance, check date and source IDs in `records[].notes`. If dominance remains unclear, use `dominance_unresolved_retained`. Neither state is a user exception.
